## Decided

- "untold story" = Stories Untold, `/Users/ludovicautin/Dev/stories-untold`, per Ludovic (relayed by the coordinator,
  2026-11-04); primary reference with Prism, MolViewStories secondary.
- (lane, for Ludovic to confirm: README §8 D2-D10) the Focus as the one selection; fixed regions instead of free
  docking; the inspector replaces Ingredient Options / Object Properties / Table Options; the warm accent; paper and
  ink; Append › Exosome appends; Save becomes Export and placeholders become greyed commands with a reason; one
  Find-a-structure card; a dark depth ramp.
- (lane) A Focus circle in the Recipe view takes the soft accent fill and the accent ring: measured, the ring alone
  falls to 2.65:1 over depth 2, HIV's ingredient colour (WCAG 1.4.11 needs 3:1); this is legacy's yellow fill and orange stroke in one
  colour.

## Learned

- The real recipes are mostly gaps: HIV's 26 ingredients all have count and molarity 0 and no UniProt, its 6 surface
  ones a principal axis of 0,0,0, 18 of 26 structures are `.pdb` files from the cellPACK_data repository; Mpn's 180 have no UniProt and no label, and 98
  of its structure names are neither PDB ids nor files.
  Curation is the main task, so problems became a principle (P5).
- `color-mix()` tokens cannot be read for contrast by eye; resolving them in Chromium and measuring is cheap
  (`check.mjs`).
- The depth colours must come from the legacy oracle, not be recomputed: d3 v7's HCL from a white endpoint gives
  lavender where legacy (d3 v4) gives teal-grey.
- `file://` blocks `fetch()` of local JSON: the sample data ships as a generated script.
- WebFetch summarises pages through a small model; only quotes re-fetched and confirmed are cited, the rest (Blender,
  Apple HIG, Material 3) are listed as not citable.
